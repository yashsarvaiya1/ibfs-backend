# Document PDF contract

Settings offers Classic (black and white, serif title) and Modern (navy, sans-serif). Both use the same accounting values and A4 pagination. Choosing a template changes presentation only.

The renderer waits for fonts and uploaded images, measures the actual header, footer, rows and summary, and creates physical sheets. Each sheet repeats business identity, document number/date, parties, reference/payment/tax metadata, the uploaded banner or full-page letterhead, and the signature/footer when supplied. Every sheet has the same compact amount-in-words box and totals breakdown in the lower section. Their labels, borders and reserved spaces repeat; all numeric summary values and amount-in-words text stay empty on intermediate sheets. Only the final sheet fills those values. At least the final item or its final description fragment stays with the completed summary on the last sheet. The item area cannot overlap the reserved summary. Bulk printing starts each document on a fresh sheet and numbers its pages separately.

Short ordinary bills/invoices fit on one page. Long saved descriptions continue without repeating their monetary values; those values belong to the final fragment. Oversized saved notes/terms can continue across sheets before the reserved final item and summary. Very large headers or tax/charge breakdowns that cannot fit safely produce a layout error instead of silently clipping data. Full-page letterhead settings reserve space above/below the content; preview the uploaded design when adjusting those settings.

Only stored business/contact/document values and calculations from available numeric inputs are printed. Missing contact/profile fields, HSN, signature, terms, place of supply and reverse-charge status are omitted. An empty preview still shows the structural amount and words placeholders without inventing values. Explicit reverse-charge No is distinct from unspecified. A missing line amount is derived from available quantity and rate; unavailable inputs stay blank. Stored document totals remain authoritative for fast entry and historical documents; a visible adjustment reconciles a complete item breakdown to that amount when necessary. No preview customer, address, products, tax rate or amount is invented: Settings preview uses the latest active saved document of that type, or an empty layout.

Optional place-of-supply and reverse-charge fields support document particulars described in [CBIC Rule 46](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001). They do not infer tax treatment, generate IRNs or submit tax returns. Document-wide calculations remain the default; optional item mode prints each saved line’s rates and aggregates rounded line taxes by name/rate in the final-page breakdown. Supplier invoice numbers and explicit supply classifications print only when provided. Applicability and populated document particulars must be confirmed for the transaction; these layouts are not a blanket GST/e-invoicing compliance claim.

Validation completed locally: both templates with short and long bills/invoices, unbranded/empty layouts, header banners, full-page letterheads, transparent signatures, very long descriptions/notes, bulk documents, real-data preview and tax-detail edits. Rendered pages were inspected with Poppler and their text checked for complete items and final-page-only totals. Rendering also passed inside the non-root backend Docker image.

Visual references reviewed: [Zoho Simple invoice example](https://www.zoho.com/invoice/templates/images/invoices/simple.pdf), [Zoho PDF layout controls](https://www.zoho.com/nl/invoice/help/settings/templates.html), and [phiReport form examples, pages 134 and 138](https://3phi.solutions/phiReport/help/pdf/phiReport.pdf). The final-page-only values and repeated blank amount/words placeholders follow the requested IBFS contract, not a claim that every reference uses that exact convention.
